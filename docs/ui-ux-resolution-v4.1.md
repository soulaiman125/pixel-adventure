This review compares the Hybrid UI candidate with the v4 audit at `e2b627a`. The [original audit](ui-ux-audit-v4.1.md) and its issue IDs are the baseline. The candidate is served locally at [127.0.0.1:4240](http://127.0.0.1:4240/) and the [private gallery](http://127.0.0.1:4241/) uses screenshots and recordings from the running game.

## Evidence and scope

The review uses isolated browser storage and a temporary Electron profile. The release scope is local browser preview and source Electron. The source Electron runtime exercised the old Windows build’s save schema in one temporary profile, then the new app relaunched with networking disabled. General loot, online rankings and physical hardware testing remain outside the candidate. Inventory surfaces only the weapons, honing and cosmetics the game tracks.

The landing artwork is an original generated backdrop saved as `public/ui/eclipse-landing.webp`. The hero is rendered from the existing gameplay pose atlas. Bricolage Grotesque and DM Sans are bundled locally. The generation prompt was

> Original dark fantasy pixel art environment backdrop for the local 2D action-platformer Pixel Adventure: Legends of Shadows. Wide landscape game title artwork, ideally 16:9. Deliberately hand-clustered SNES-inspired pixel art with crisp, visible square pixels, sophisticated restrained palette and dramatic sense of scale. Nightfall above a remote mountain valley. A striking crimson ring eclipse sits at about 69 percent across and 30 percent down, softly lighting clustered clouds. On the right a layered silhouette of a faraway Japanese-inspired fantasy fortress and spires on a rocky cliff rises out of dark violet fog. Foreground rocks and wind-bent trees frame the lower right and lower edge, where the game will separately place its existing animated ninja sprite. Keep the left 48 percent predominantly deep navy and charcoal with very restrained cloud detail and clear negative space for HTML title and buttons, but ensure the landscape still spans the whole canvas. Dark midnight blue and dusty mulberry, a few atmospheric pink and ember sparks, strong black silhouettes. No people, no figures, no writing, no logo, no boxes, no mock interface. Pure pixel art background, no gradient banding, no vector or photorealistic treatment.

## Test results

- **Audit replay.** 289 original controls were matched to successful interactions. All 54 previously inspected screens and states were revisited. The eight broken routes were Continue, Combat Trials, Legends, the Windows link, the repository link, controller Resume, Village Portal Back, and Hero Selection Back. The expanded ledger has 60 verified entries and one explicit exclusion for an online leaderboard, which the app has no backend to support. Six audited gaps are now reachable, including lower landscape navigation, Continue, New Adventure confirmation, startup recovery, purchase review and Village fast travel. [Controls](http://127.0.0.1:4241/controls-review.csv) · [States](http://127.0.0.1:4241/states-review.csv)
- **Campaign gameplay.** The normal-input Shadow Ninja runner cleared stages 1–15 sequentially from a fresh campaign and stages 16–60 via independent stage selection. The runner observed all three attack patterns for each of the eight bosses. The first no-Dash attempt stalled at the Fire Kingdom’s Molten Citadel. A separate run completed it using the existing Dash. The control ledger also exercises all 60 stage buttons and result transitions with isolated fixtures. [Gameplay evidence](http://127.0.0.1:4241/campaign-evidence.json)
- **Responsive layouts.** Desktop and tablet checks covered 1920 × 1080, 1600 × 900, 1366 × 768, 1280 × 720, 1024 × 768 and 1180 × 820. Phone emulation covered 390 × 844, 844 × 390, 932 × 430, 667 × 375 and 568 × 320. Every Hub target passed hit testing. The independent touch suite also checked 320 px and 360 px portrait widths, release and cancellation, buffered jumps, attack while moving, rotation and preference persistence. At 568 × 320 the boss playfield measured 210 px, the touch dock 55 px, and the boss strip 35 px. Each preset uses the available 46 px square target at this width. [Responsive grid](http://127.0.0.1:4241/#responsive)
- **Save and offline verification.** A returning browser profile kept its active result bytes during Continue and Cancel. Confirmed restart cleared active totals and retained both unlocks and records. A legacy Windows save fixture, campaign milestones, a paid Shadow upgrade, hero selection, and audio settings persisted in Electron. All 60 stages loaded offline with zero external gameplay requests.
- **Build and regression verification.** The Vite production build and 50 Node tests passed. Browser smoke, touch, Ninja and Legends browser, Hybrid UI acceptance, Electron and performance runs cover the UI, gameplay, saves and local startup. Edge device emulation is the source for browser touch and controller evidence.
- **Contrast.** Core palette pairs measure 13.88:1 for primary text on panels, 8.86:1 for secondary text on panels, 7.24:1 for primary-button text, and 8.16:1 for cyan focus on raised surfaces. These measurements cover the token pairs and complement the actual game captures.

## Remaining verification

Physical phones, real-world display cutouts, controller hardware and screen-reader speech output remain unverified. Simulated cutouts passed at 568 × 320, 844 × 390, and 932 × 430. Browser-based offline startup was tested as a recoverable loading failure. The desktop run used the offline source Electron app. External link clicks were checked with intercepted GitHub destinations so the original browser tab and exact handoff URLs could be verified within the private review.
