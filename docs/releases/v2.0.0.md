## Pixel Adventure 2.0

The free platformer now includes 30 stages across seven worlds. Explore Poison Jungle and Thunder Fortress, dodge moving lasers, saws, crushers, rising lava, and poison traps, and face the three-phase Poison Queen and Thunder Titan.

- Play in your browser with keyboard or customizable multitouch controls, including a left-handed layout.
- Listen to seven original world themes and set music and effects volume independently.
- Retry from your checkpoint, track best times and no-death clears, and find stages by world in Level Select.
- Keep existing progress when upgrading from version 1.0.0.

### Windows x64

Close any open Pixel Adventure window and run `Pixel-Adventure-Setup-2.0.0-x64.exe`. You can install it over v1.0.0. Launch Pixel Adventure from the Start menu or desktop to play all 30 stages offline.

The installer is unsigned. Windows SmartScreen may display a warning.

The Windows installer and browser game are free to play.
