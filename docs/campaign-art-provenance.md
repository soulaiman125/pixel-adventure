# Campaign background artwork

Six original background plates were generated with ImageGen for the private 3.0 candidate. The game uses 576 × 384 WebP copies encoded at quality 0.93 after nearest-neighbor sampling. Character, enemy, boss, terrain and effect sprites are authored in the repository. The source PNGs remain in the local generated-image directory.

## canyon

Saved game asset `public/worlds/canyon.webp`. Original generated file `exec-85898adc-34c9-420f-b228-c04afb10aad5.png`.

```
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: full-bleed panoramic background plate for an original 2D side-view pixel art platform game. Scene: Sunset Canyon, layered warm terracotta mesas, distant worn stone arches, tiny desert shrubs and a gold-pink sunset, violet canyon shadows. Style: refined 16-bit console pixel art, deliberate clusters and dithered shadow transitions, beautifully detailed environmental surfaces, coherent native pixel density, clear depth separation. Wide 3:2 landscape composition. Keep the bottom third low contrast, simple and atmospheric to leave room for the playable hero, enemies and platforms overlaid by the game. Include foreground-free distant scenery only. No characters, coins, HUD, text, logos, frames or gameplay ground surface. Daylight and color are appropriate to this world. Use a cool dark outline palette matching a charcoal ninja with a crimson scarf. No existing IP.
```
