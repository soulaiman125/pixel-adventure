    How to Play

    Reach the finish flag to unlock the next level. Every coin earns 100 points.

    Move
    A D or ← →
    Jump
    W, ↑, or Space. Hold for a higher jump.
    Drop
    S or ↓ through floating platforms.
    Katana
    X or J, or tap Attack. Chain two quick cuts into a heavy finisher by pressing again during recovery.
    Charge
    Hold X beyond the first cut, then release to send a sword wave.
    Shuriken
    C or tap ✦. Throw again after a short cooldown.
    Dash
    Z. One air dash per landing. Hazards still hurt.
    Sprint
    Hold Left Shift to run faster on the ground.
    Techniques
    V Cyclone and F Execution use cooldowns. Execution needs an exposed target.
    Parry and air moves
    Q blocks a frontal projectile or a cyan-marked melee strike. Follow with Katana. Dodge traps and body contact. Press Down + Katana in the air for a dive. Jump off a marked wall once before landing.
    Retry
    R or Retry Checkpoint in Pause. Keeps coins and counts as a death.
    Touch
    Hold an arrow to move. Press Jump with your other thumb.

    Stomp slimes from above. Falls and side hits send you to the latest checkpoint with your coins.
